Nook privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Nook is a virtual study library for students of Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB), built as a student project by Sadman Mahmood. This page explains what Nook keeps and why.

What Nook stores

  • Your Google sign-in: your IUB email address and the name on your Google account. Nook uses this only to check that you are an IUB student and to suggest a name for your avatar.
  • Your avatar and where you are in the library: your chosen name, look, seat and timer. Other signed-in students see this while you are in the library. It is not stored after you leave.
  • Files and notes you share: stored so the people you shared them with can open them. Shares sent to one person are only visible to you and that person.
  • On your own device only: your tasks, study minutes, streak and settings stay in your browser and are never sent to Nook’s server.

What Nook does not do

  • Nook does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • Nook has no ads and no tracking.
  • Nook does not read your Gmail, Drive or anything else in your Google account. It only receives your name and email address.

Where the data lives

Nook runs on Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, in the Mumbai region) and Cloudflare Pages (the website). Sign-in uses Google.

Removing your data

You can remove anything you shared from the Shared files panel at any time. To have your account and everything linked to it deleted, contact the developer at the address below.
